UART · Interrupts · DMA · Cache

Your CPU is fast. So why is it waiting?

Let's send one message, Blink 10, from a small board to a laptop. Easy? It works on the first try, and then we notice the CPU just standing there waiting. Each time we fix a problem, a new one appears: interrupts, DMA, caches. Follow the robots, one step at a time. Every number was measured on a real STM32 board.

Everything we measured

STM32 with a Cortex-M7 core at 520 MHz, UART at 115200 baud, Debug build. "Pulses" are CPU clock cycles, counted with the core's cycle counter DWT->CYCCNT. One character on the wire = 10 bits = 45,120 pulses.
